Frame

Hand-bent tubing, tacked in the jig and welded on this table. Every frame is drawn around the rider who ordered it.

Tubing
1¾ in DOM steel
Rake
38°
Stretch
4 in up, 2 in out

Engine

The V-twin comes in on the hoist and sits low in the cradle, so the bike's weight stays down where you can feel it.

Displacement
117 cu in
Finish
Polished fins, chrome covers

Station 3 of 8

Rear wheel

A wide rear tire gives the bike its stance. The wheel is machined from one billet and laced to a chrome rotor.

Tire
300 mm section
Wheel
18 in billet

Station 4 of 8

Front end

A stretched wide-glide fork and a tall, thin front wheel. Long enough to look right, short enough to steer.

Fork
6 in over stock
Wheel
21 in, single rotor

Station 5 of 8

Tank

Stretched to follow the backbone. Candy apple over a silver base, with the flames laid out by hand and riveted trim.

Capacity
3.6 gal
Paint
Candy apple, ghost flames

Station 6 of 8

Seat and fender

A solo seat in black leather with red stitching. The fender is rolled to wrap the rear tire with a finger's width to spare.

Seat
Hand-stitched leather
Fender
Rolled 14-gauge steel

Station 7 of 8

Exhaust

Two-into-two drag pipes, bent to clear your boot and cut short so you hear the engine, not the muffler.

Pipes
1¾ in chrome
Tips
Slash cut

Station 8 of 8

Bars and light

Ape hangers set to your reach and a seven-inch headlight. After a shakedown ride, it rolls off the table.

Bars
16 in apes
Headlight
7 in chrome bucket
